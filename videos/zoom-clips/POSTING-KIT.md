## Why these should outperform the old posts

- **Frame 1 is a human face plus a curiosity hook**, not a logo. The logo only appears after 3 s.
- **Third-party proof:** an architect saying "You should sell this" and "Congrats… really amazing" is more
  believable than anything we say about ourselves.
- **Captions are burned in**, so the videos work on mute, which is how most FB and IG feed views happen.
- **Each clip carries one idea in under 40 s.** Short-form reach depends on watch-through.
